Roomly support

The chore schedule for shared flats.

Getting started

  • Start a room. Sign in with Apple, enter your name and choose Create a room.
  • Invite your flatmates. The 6-character invite code is on the Room tab. Flatmates choose Join with a code and type it in.
  • Add your chores. On the Week tab, choose Add a chore and pick the days it needs doing.

Common questions

How is the schedule decided?
Roomly deals each week's chores so everyone gets the same number, give or take one, and keeps chores off the days you said you cannot do when it can.

When does next week's schedule come out?
On Saturday morning. Until Monday, any flatmate can shuffle it up to three times, and everyone sees who did.

Why are the earlier days of this week empty?
Chores are only dealt from the day they were added. Days already past stay blank.

I'm not getting notifications.
Open the Room tab and check Notifications. If it says they are turned off, switch them on for Roomly in iOS Settings.

Someone did my chore for me. How do they mark it?
On the Today tab, they press and hold your chore and confirm.

How do I change the days I can't do?
Room tab, then Days I can't do. The change applies to schedules dealt from then on.

How do I delete my account?
Room tab, then Delete my data. This is permanent.
