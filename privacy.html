Privacy policy

Roomly, the chore schedule for shared flats. Last updated November 23, 2026.

Roomly keeps only what it needs to show a chore schedule to the people who live together. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

What Roomly stores

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Apple. Roomly receives an account identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address or Apple's private relay address. Roomly never sees your Apple password.
  • Your name. The name you enter, shown to the people in your room.
  • Your room. The room's name and invite code, its chores, the weekly schedule, who each chore was assigned to, which chores were marked done and by whom, and nudges and swap requests sent between flatmates.
  • Your preferences. The weekdays you said you cannot do chores, which chores you said you like or would rather not do, any dates you said you are away, the room's reminder time and its time zone.
  • A notification address for your phone. If you allow notifications, Roomly stores the token that lets it send them to your device.

Roomly does not collect your location, contacts, photos or anything else on your phone.

Who can see it

Your name, your chores, whether they were done, and any dates you said you are away are visible to the people in your room, and to nobody else. Which chores you like or would rather not do is visible only to you. Anyone who has your room's invite code can join the room, so share the code only with your flatmates.

Where it is kept

The information is stored with Supabase, a database hosting provider, on servers in the United States. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple's Push Notification service, which receive the notification text and your device's token in order to deliver it.

How long it is kept

Your information is kept for as long as you have an account. Schedules and chore history stay with the room while it has members.

Deleting your information

In the app, open the Room tab and choose Delete my data. This removes you from your room, permanently deletes your name, preferences, notification token and account, and ends the app's Sign in with Apple link. Chores you completed stay in the room's history without your name. You can also ask for deletion by email at the address below.

Children

Roomly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
